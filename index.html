How to resize a photo for a Pakistan online form

Pick the preset that matches your form, choose your photo, and download the result. The tool crops from the center to the right proportions, then lowers the JPEG quality until the file fits under the limit. For a clean result, start with a plain background and a face that fills most of the frame.

Applying for admission, a government job, NADRA, or a passport? Most portals reject files that are too large or the wrong shape. Resize here first, then upload.

Is it safe?

Yes. The resizing runs entirely in your browser. Your CNIC photo and signature are never uploaded to a server.
